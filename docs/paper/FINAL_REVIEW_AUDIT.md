# Final skeptical reviewer audit

## Assessment

The revision substantially strengthens experimental control, dataset provenance
and claim discipline. It is a defensible bounded prototype study once its
recorded verification gates pass. It is **not evidence of a major new indexing
algorithm, an optimal adaptive selector, general Dolt superiority or independent
reproduction**. A selective systems journal could still reject it for limited
novelty and generality despite the improved engineering evidence.

## Novelty

Content addressing, path copying, Merkle comparison, changesets and SQLite
transactions are established. Putting them together in Python is incremental
engineering. The more credible contribution is a common durable representation,
explicit diff-path controls, semantic verification and a transparent trade-off
study. The title now reflects that scope. The calibrated winner always selects
log within the tested range, which weakens any claim that adaptation itself is
the central validated contribution. A new selector would need new calibration
and untouched testing; it should not be invented after seeing these results.

## Methodology and uncertainty

Positive changes: disjoint calibration/validation seeds, frozen choice before
validation, controlled primary N/H/payload/geometry, a staged sensitivity design,
new public sources and repeated local scale observations. Every planned case
and configuration is retained. Correctness uses observed output/state hashes.

Remaining weaknesses: only two calibration seeds and three held-out seeds per
profile; one Windows host under substantial background memory use; resident
state and filesystem caching; no cold-cache control, randomised machine sample
or full factorial. The 18 held-out profile/seed cases are not 18 unrelated data
domains. Three-seed ranges are descriptive. They cannot support population
confidence or a broad crossover claim. Many exploratory comparisons are made
without a significance claim. The original Linux intervals concern fixed
histories, not machine populations.

The old threshold failure and dense-case inconclusiveness remain. The new
calibrated result does not erase them. Lower forced-path medians are noisy
hindsight references, so path agreement is descriptive rather than classification
accuracy against a known optimum.

## Fairness and resources

Within Revon, representation/history/persistence/output semantics are controlled.
Across Revon and Dolt, API/CLI and language/runtime boundaries remain different.
New phase instrumentation reveals that most Dolt latency is in the native
command interval, but cannot distinguish startup from native execution or
establish tree-engine superiority. Dolt also provides substantially richer
features. A server/reused-process comparison is a different experiment, not a
correction that can be inferred from current measurements.

RSS and CPU cover whole workers and their sampled descendants, including oracle
construction and verification. RSS is not product-only memory. OS process I/O
bytes are not database logical reads or direct physical-disk traffic. Sampling
can miss peaks and short-lived processes. The monitor diagnostic has off before
on; it demonstrates variability rather than causally isolating overhead.

## Generalisability

Wine and CO2 broaden the public-source base beyond TLC. Wine corrections are
generated; CO2 contributes one observed, filtered publication history. Repeating
that sequence in three repositories improves timing description, not independent
history coverage. The excluded header-only revision and preserved unnamed CSV
fields restrict domain interpretation. No atmospheric or wine-quality findings
are made. Repeated scale runs share the initial synthetic distribution and
cover two sizes. They support conditional observations, not a scaling law.

## Reproducibility and presentation

The revision retains frozen sources, raw observations, complete accounting,
licence/acquisition metadata and portable read-only replay. Checksums and
semantic replay validate internal consistency; they do not recreate elapsed
timing or replace an independent team's reproduction. Exact source snapshots
provide provenance even though manuscript links follow `main`.

The related-work table and takeaways improve navigation. Detailed material has
moved into Appendix B, although the paper remains long. PDF is the reference
layout and Word is an editable companion with different pagination. Historical
tables/figures must remain unchanged beyond citation/table renumbering; the
verification gate checks this. Final visual checks and author/corresponding-author
review are necessary. No journal submission or email is performed by this
revision. Subsequent authorised Git publication is recorded in the verification
report and remains separate from author approval for submission.

## Remaining research work, outside the completed bounded revision

1. Establish a contribution beyond the current scalar rule, or explicitly
   position the work as a negative/conditional adaptive-selection study.
2. Collect additional independently sampled observed histories and hardware.
3. Evaluate another Dolt interface if engine-level questions are pursued.
4. Use randomised monitor conditions or external instrumentation for a causal
   overhead study, and richer OS tooling for operation-level resource questions.
5. Obtain independent-party reproduction and corresponding-author approval.

These are open research limits, not unperformed items silently substituted for
the 93-case experiment requested and completed in this revision.

were ignored output; calibration scores were copied; prefix provenance lacked
comparison with archived production code. Maintained tools now enforce required
versioned assets, independent raw-score/chronology checks, retained source hashes
and AST agreement. An ignored local review document is excluded. Original
experimental collections and canonical manuscripts remain unchanged. See
[REVIEW_REMEDIATION.md](REVIEW_REMEDIATION.md) for the repairs and 25 regression
controls. The current verification report records actual full-gate and clean
