# Revon verification of all 68 references

Checked on 26 October 2026. All 68 cited source identities were confirmed: 53 Crossref DOI records, 2 DataCite DOI records, and 13 official original-source records. No fabricated source was identified in this check. Every numbered entry in the corrected Word file now has at least one active hyperlink to its confirmed source.

This is an existence and bibliographic-identity audit. It does not establish that a source supports a particular claim in Revon, certify research quality, or make every entry a peer-reviewed paper. Titles, named authors, publication context and available dates were compared. Web resources and preprints are labelled separately. The manuscript wording and numbering were not rewritten.

The linked DOI is usually a stable publication landing page, not necessarily a free PDF. Paywalls or institutional access requirements are not evidence that a source is unreal.
